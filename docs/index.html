STEP 1 · INPUT

Coordinates are the query.

The network receives points in space-time. A classical PINN does not march the solution forward like a finite-difference solver; it represents the entire field as a function.

\[z=(x,t)\quad\longmapsto\quad u_{\theta}(z)\]
THE MATHEMATICAL CONTRACT

A PINN must satisfy a PDE and select the intended solution.

Governing equation
\[ u_{tt}(x,t)-c^{2}\Delta u(x,t)=s(x,t), \qquad (x,t)\in\Omega\times(0,T]. \]
(1)
Neural ansatz
\[ u_{\theta}:\Omega\times[0,T]\to\mathbb{R}, \qquad (x,t)\mapsto u_{\theta}(x,t). \]
(2)
Physics residual
\[ \mathcal{R}_{\theta}(x,t) =\partial_{tt}u_{\theta}(x,t) -c^{2}\Delta u_{\theta}(x,t)-s(x,t). \]
(3)

The residual measures local consistency with the governing equation. Initial and boundary conditions are still required to identify the intended physical solution.

Training objective
\[ \mathcal{L}(\theta) =\lambda_{r}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PDE}} +\lambda_{\mathrm{IC}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{IC}} +\lambda_{\mathrm{BC}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BC}} +\lambda_{d}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{data}}. \]
+ + +
PDE residual
\[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PDE}}=\frac{1}{N_r}\sum_{i=1}^{N_r}\left|\mathcal{R}_{\theta}(x_i,t_i)\right|^2\]

Collocation points interrogate the differential equation throughout the interior of the space-time domain.

WHEN PINNs ARE NATURAL

Physics is information.

PINNs are especially interesting when governing equations and partial observations must be combined inside one differentiable model.

inverse parameterssparse datadifferentiable surrogatephysics constraints
WHEN TO BE CAREFUL

A residual is not a certificate.

High frequency, long-time propagation, stiffness, competing loss terms, and poor sampling can produce low-loss solutions with large field error.

spectral biasoptimization collapsesampling pathologyboundary reflections
A NUMERICAL QUESTION

Should a neural solver exist here at all?

If a classical method is cheap, stable, accurate, and already differentiable enough for the task, replacing it with a PINN may add optimization cost without adding scientific value.

DIRECT ANSWER · QUESTION A

My main conclusion from these experiments is that physics informed learning cannot be judged by the loss alone.

It should be judged by whether the learned dynamics survive numerical checks, independent seeds, new trajectories, longer horizons, and uncertainty tests.
