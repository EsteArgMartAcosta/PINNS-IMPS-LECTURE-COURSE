04 · WHAT CHANGED IN MY MENTAL MODEL

Four weeks, four shifts

Not a list of papers — a sequence of increasingly structural answers to the same question.

W1

Physics can become a loss.

PINNs turn a differential equation into an optimization objective.

But optimization sees only what representation and sampling expose.
W2

Time ordering matters.

Causality is not merely data order; it can reshape the training objective.

Low training residual does not guarantee the right temporal dynamics.
W3

The domain is part of the model.

Boundaries, frequency representation, and sampling change what problem the network effectively learns.

Wave problems expose these choices brutally.
W4

Physics can become architecture.

NeuSA embeds a spectral state and numerical evolution directly into the model.

Then integration error and transfer become first-class questions.
